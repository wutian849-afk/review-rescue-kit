## Why Customers Don't Leave Reviews

- 96% of satisfied customers never leave a review — **they just don't think about it**
- Customers who had a bad experience leave a review 3x more often
- The solution: **make it easy + give them a reason**

This pack contains 10+ templates to ask happy customers for reviews across every channel.

[Your Name]

## How to Create Your Google Review Link

**Use this format:**

```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID
```

To find your Place ID:
1. Go to Google's Place ID Finder: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/javascript/examples/places-placeid-finder
2. Search for your business
3. Copy the Place ID
4. Replace `YOUR_PLACE_ID` in the URL above

**Alternative (easier):**
Use this shortener: `http://g.page/r/review/YOUR_BUSINESS_NAME`

Test your link before using it!

## Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| ✅ Ask within 24 hours of service | ❌ Ask when the customer is unhappy |
| ✅ Keep it short and simple | ❌ Make it complicated |
| ✅ Give a direct link (one click) | ❌ Make them search for your listing |
| ✅ Ask happy customers only | ❌ Ask every single customer |
| ✅ Respond to every review you get | ❌ Ignore reviews — good or bad |
| ✅ Say thank you for positive reviews | ❌ Be pushy or demanding |

---

## What to Do With New 5-Star Reviews

When you get a new positive review:

1. **Reply within 48 hours**
2. **Be specific**: mention something from their review
3. **Personalize**: use their name
4. **Keep it warm**: show genuine appreciation

**Sample reply to positive review:**

```
Hi [Name], thank you so much for your kind words! We're thrilled you enjoyed 
[specific thing they mentioned]. Our team works hard to provide great service 
and it means a lot to hear when we get it right. Hope to see you again soon!

Best,
[Your Name]
```
