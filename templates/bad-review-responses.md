## CATEGORY 1: Legitimate Bad Reviews (Customer Had a Real Issue)
*Use these when the customer's complaint is valid.*

### #1 Service Was Slow
**Scenario:** Customer waited too long for service.
```

time. 

Here's what I'd like to do: [specific compensation: discount/refund/free service]. 
Please email me at [email] so I can make this right for you personally.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## CATEGORY 2: Fake Reviews / Competitor Sabotage
*Use these when you suspect the review is not from a real customer.*

### #21 No Record of Customer
**Scenario:** The name doesn't match any customer in your system.
```
Hi [Name], I've searched our records and can't find any booking, order, or service 
under your name. Could you please email me at [email] with more details — your order 
number, the date of visit, or a description of what happened?

If we can't verify your visit, I'd appreciate it if you'd consider updating or removing 
your review. We take all feedback seriously, but we want to ensure it reflects genuine 
experiences.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### #22 Review Describes Competitor's Service
**Scenario:** The complaint mentions features/products you don't offer.
```
Hi [Name], thank you for your feedback, though I believe there may be a mix-up with 
another business. The [specific issue] you described doesn't match our services or 
products. We don't offer [mentioned service].

Could you please email me at [email] with more details so I can look into this? If 
there's been a mistake, I'd appreciate it if you'd update your review accordingly.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### #23 Multiple 1-Star Reviews in Same Week

[Your Name]

## BONUS: Short Template (For Mobile / Quick Responses)

**If you're short on time, use this streamlined version:**

| Situation | Reply |
|-----------|-------|
| Legitimate complaint | "Hi [Name], you're right and I'm sorry. I'm fixing this and would love to make it up to you. Please email [email]." |
| Fake review | "Hi [Name], we have no record of your visit. Please email [email] with your details so we can look into this." |
| Already resolved | "Hi [Name], glad we could resolve this! If everything is good now, we'd appreciate a review update." |
| General acknowledgment | "Hi [Name], thanks for the feedback. We're always improving. Please reach out to [email] if you'd like to discuss further." |

## Usage Instructions

1. **Copy the template** that best matches your situation
2. **Replace** `[Name]`, `[email]`, `[number]`, `[$amount]`, and specific details
3. **Post as the business owner** on Google Reviews (reply publicly)
4. **Be genuine** — customers can tell when you're just copying text
5. **Respond within 48 hours** — faster is always better
6. **DO NOT** argue with the customer publicly — take it to email/phone
